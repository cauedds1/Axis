Overview

FrameRent is a production-ready multi-tenant SaaS platform for equipment rental businesses.

The platform combines inventory management, smart scheduling, financial tools, CRM, AI-generated contracts, and a built-in storefront website builder that allows every tenant to have their own branded rental website.

Status:

• 100% completed product
• pre-revenue
• live deployment
• ready for global scale

This is a clean-slate SaaS acquisition with no legacy customers or pricing.
