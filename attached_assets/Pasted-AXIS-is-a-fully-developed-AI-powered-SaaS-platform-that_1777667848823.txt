AXIS is a fully developed AI-powered SaaS platform that combines personal productivity and business expense management into a single scalable system.

The platform is structured as two independent products sharing the same infrastructure, allowing multiple monetization strategies and use cases.

? AXIS PERSONAL (B2C)

An AI-powered personal assistant for managing finances, tasks, habits, and schedule through natural interactions.

Users can interact with the system using:

Text (chat interface)
Voice commands (transcribed and processed with AI)
Photos (receipt scanning)
PDFs (bank statements and bills)
Core capabilities:

Expense tracking with AI categorization (manual, voice, receipt, PDF)
Financial dashboard with reports, trends, and goals
Task and habit management with performance tracking and discipline scoring
Smart scheduling with AI-based time suggestions
AI chat assistant with full access to user data
WhatsApp integration for sending expenses, voice notes, and documents
Automated email alerts (weekly summary, bills, goals, inactivity)
? Designed for individual users who want automation instead of manual tracking.

? AXIS BUSINESS (B2B)

A multi-tenant expense management system designed for teams and organizations.

Companies can manage employees, track expenses, and control financial workflows in a centralized environment.

Core capabilities:

Organization management (multi-tenant) with roles (admin/member)
Expense submission via WhatsApp (photo, voice, text)
Approval workflow (pending → approved/rejected with comments)
Receipt analysis with AI
Accounts payable & receivable tracking
Corporate cards management
Cashflow overview (income vs expenses)
Advanced reporting + Excel export
Public shareable reports (no login required)
Employee portal for expense submission and tracking
? Designed for companies needing lightweight, automated expense control without complex ERP systems.
